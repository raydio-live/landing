# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Crew leads and teammates who coordinate live: field ops, event and venue crews,
warehouse and delivery teams, coaching staff, and friends and family on the move.
They use Raydio on phones, often one-handed, while doing something else.

## Product Purpose

Raydio is a realtime push-to-talk app: open a channel, hold to talk, and be heard
instantly. Success is a first channel live in under a minute, with zero hardware
and zero call setup.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, confident. The radio minus the hardware: direct, reliable, quietly
premium. Liner-clean restraint.

## Anti-references

Dark editorial panels, serif-italic headline accents, mono kickers on every
section, marquee tickers, giant watermark footers, ghost cards (1px border plus
soft shadow), identical card grids repeated down the page.

## Design Principles

- Instant over complete: one action per screen, no setup theater.
- Show the channel, not the chrome: product surface first, decoration last.
- Quiet confidence: restraint, generous whitespace, a restrained palette:
  warm paper neutrals, slate ink (from the logo mark) for text and primary
  buttons, denim blue for identity, and a coral "on air" signal reserved for
  live and talking states.
- Thumb-first: big targets, hold-to-talk ergonomics, legible at a glance.
- Private by default: member-only channels, no public-feed energy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: body text ≥4.5:1 against its background, visible focus rings,
keyboard-reachable controls, 44px minimum touch targets, reduced-motion
alternatives for all animation.
